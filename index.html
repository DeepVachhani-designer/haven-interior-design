01 About us

Thoughtful design, built around you.

Every room starts with a conversation — what you need it to hold, and how you want it to feel.

We're a small studio based in Bengaluru, working with clients across India, that takes on a deliberately small number of projects each year — because good interiors take real attention. Every brief starts with how a space is actually lived in, not just how it photographs.

From first sketch to final styling, the same people stay on your project the whole way through. Materials are chosen for how they age, not just how they look on day one — the goal is a home that gets better with use.

03 How we work

A process built for fewer surprises.

The same four stages for every project, whether it's one room or a full renovation.

01

Discovery & brief. A conversation about how you live, your budget range, and what the space needs to do for you.

02

Concept & design. Layouts, materials and a full concept board, refined together before anything is ordered.

03

Sourcing & execution. We manage vendors, contractors and site visits, so you're seeing progress, not problems.

04

Styling & handover. Furniture placed, details styled, and a walkthrough before you move back in.
